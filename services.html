Safety & Compliance

Safety Inspections & Testing

Keeping your electrical systems safe and compliant is not optional — it protects your people, your equipment, and your business. Our licensed inspectors conduct thorough safety assessments and diagnostic testing across your entire electrical infrastructure.

We test to OSHA and local regulatory standards and provide detailed reports with clear findings and recommended actions. We also offer arc flash analysis, grounding system testing, and thermographic scanning to identify hidden issues before they become hazards.

  • Full facility electrical safety audits
  • Arc flash analysis and labeling
  • Grounding and bonding system testing
  • Thermographic (infrared) scanning
  • Switchgear and circuit breaker testing
  • Detailed inspection reports and action plans

Protection Systems

Grounding & Lightning Protection

In the Dominican Republic, lightning strikes and grounding failures are among the most common causes of electrical equipment damage. A proper grounding and lightning protection system is essential — not just for compliance, but for the long-term health of your infrastructure.

We design, install, and test grounding systems and lightning protection solutions tailored to your facility's layout and risk profile.

  • Grounding system design and installation
  • Ground resistance testing
  • Lightning rod and surge protection systems
  • Bonding and equipotential systems
