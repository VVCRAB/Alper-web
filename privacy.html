Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Alper Power Services ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly — when you submit our contact form, we collect your first name, last name, email address, subject, and message. If you sign up for updates, we collect your email address.
  • Information collected automatically — like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically log standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

We do not currently use analytics or advertising trackers. If that changes in the future, we will update this policy to describe what's collected and why.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form
  • To send updates, if you've opted in through our newsletter signup
  • To maintain the security of our website and prevent spam or abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

To operate this website, we rely on a small number of trusted third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers messages submitted through our contact form. See their privacy policy.
  • hCaptcha — helps us detect and block spam submissions. See their privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — used to display our location. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Cookies

Embedded content, such as the Google Map and hCaptcha widget on our Contact page, may set their own cookies as part of how they function. We don't control these directly — they're governed by the linked privacy policies above.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, and newsletter email addresses for as long as you remain subscribed. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Wherever you're visiting from, you can:

  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send
  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information

To make a request, email us at alperpowerservices@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at alperpowerservices@gmail.com.
